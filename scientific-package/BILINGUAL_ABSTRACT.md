## English

We study two amplitude-classification problems derived from the *Cyclicity
Support-Fusion Atlas* and one externally motivated ordinary coefficient-Bautin
problem. First, we reconstruct the polynomial-amplitude support
identity within the paper: a formal moment transform splits the twisted de
Rham quotient into Kummer channels, a falling-factorial Vandermonde argument
computes cyclic rank, and Fourier inversion identifies the same channels with
reduced primitive values on a generic fibre. This yields an exact
all-amplitude classification for nondegenerate Dickson phases \((a\ne0)\):
every primitive
has a free paired-module expansion and its rank is \(q=2N+\varepsilon\), so odd
degree realizes precisely the even ranks while even degree realizes every
rank. Second, for the exceptional Ritt collision \(P=(x^4+x)^3\), we exhibit
three explicit rank-three fused modules; together with two composition
channels they partition all eleven non-trace residues and classify every
polynomial amplitude. Third, for every degree-\(d\) phase, every zero-cycle and
every polynomial perturbation of arbitrary fixed degree, we prove that the ordinary
coefficient Bautin ideal is generated by Melnikov orders one through
\(d-1\). The bound follows from an all-order Lagrange--Bürmann identity,
monodromy injectivity of differentiation, and a Cayley--Hamilton recurrence;
in particular, the explicitly defined degree-box index satisfies
\(b_{\mathrm{coeff}}(m)\le m-1\). If the terse \(b(m)\) in the cited source
uses this ordinary coefficient convention, it obeys the same bound. The
\(d-1\) cutoff is sharp on the complexified cycle module; for integral cycles
we prove only the lower witness \(d/p\), where \(p\) is the least prime divisor
of \(d\). As supporting infrastructure, we identify inverse
support with the minimal order of an operator annihilating the complete
rapid-decay period system and give corrected block bounds for standard
ray-cycles. The contribution is a theorem candidate: the support proof follows
the Atlas architecture, the novelty search is bounded, parameter content is
proved equivalent to the ordinary local Taylor-coefficient ideal, and
independent specialist review remains open.

**Keywords:** polynomial exponential periods; twisted de Rham modules;
inverse-at-infinity support; Dickson polynomials; polynomial monodromy;
creative telescoping; zero-cycles; Bautin ideals; Melnikov functions.

## 繁體中文

本文研究兩個由《循環性支撐融合圖譜》衍生的振幅分類問題，以及一個由外部文獻提出的
通常係數 Bautin 問題。首先，我們在文中重建
多項式振幅的支撐恆等式：形式矩變換把扭曲德拉姆商分解為 Kummer 通道，下降階乘的
Vandermonde 論證計算循環秩，而 Fourier 反演則把同一批通道辨識為一般纖維上原函數值
的約化張成空間。由此得到非退化（\(a\ne0\)）Dickson 相位的全振幅精確分類：
每個原函數都有唯一
的自由配對模分解，且其秩為 \(q=2N+\varepsilon\)；奇數次僅實現偶數秩，偶數次則實現
所有可能的秩。其次，對例外 Ritt 碰撞 \(P=(x^4+x)^3\)，我們明確構造三個秩為三的
融合模；它們與兩個合成通道共同分割全部十一個非跡留數，因而分類每個多項式振幅。
第三，對每個 \(d\) 次相位、其任意零維週期，以及任意固定次數的多項式擾動，我們
證明通常係數 Bautin 理想由第一至第 \(d-1\) 階 Melnikov 係數生成。此界由全階
Lagrange--Bürmann 恆等式、微分在零維週期多項式積分空間上的單射性，以及
Cayley--Hamilton 遞迴推出；特別地，明確定義的次數盒指標滿足
\(b_{\mathrm{coeff}}(m)\le m-1\)。若引文中簡略記號 \(b(m)\) 採用此普通係數慣例，
則同一界限亦適用。\(d-1\) 的截斷界在複數化週期模上可達；對整係數週期，本文僅證明
下界見證 \(d/p\)，其中 \(p\) 是 \(d\) 的最小質因數。作為支撐工具，我們亦證明
逆級數支撐等於消滅完整快速衰減週期系統
之算子的最小階，並給出標準射線週期的修正塊界。以上成果目前仍屬定理候選：
支撐證明沿用圖譜的架構；文獻新穎性搜尋有界；參數內容已證明等同於通常的局部
Taylor 係數理想；且尚待獨立專家審查。

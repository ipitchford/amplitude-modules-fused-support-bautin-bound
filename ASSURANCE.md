# Claim and assurance boundary

## Established by this release

- Public availability of an anonymous unrefereed candidate and its exact
  source package.
- A deterministic internal replay receipt covering 13 command executions and
  14 byte comparisons.
- Five declared mutation controls rejected as intended.
- Ordinary and optimized publication controls with identical 9-test JUnit
  inventories.
- Byte identities for the PDF, HTML, scientific ZIP, source manifest, and
  release evidence.

## Not established by this release

- Independent specialist reconstruction or validation.
- Journal peer review, formal proof-assistant verification, or submission.
- Settled novelty or priority.
- A solution of three historically posed open problems.
- A real planar limit-cycle bound.
- Unconditional identification of the manuscript's `b_coeff(m)` with the terse
  `b(m)` used in the cited source.

The proofs should be evaluated as mathematics in an unrefereed candidate. The
replay demonstrates specified computational behaviour and package identity; it
does not certify theorem truth.
